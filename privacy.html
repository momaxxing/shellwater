Shellwater privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

The short version

Shellwater does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your game progress (unlocked dives, pearls, shells and best times).
  • Your diver's look and the name you type for your diver.
  • Whether sound is muted.

This is saved only on your own device so you can pick up where you left off. It is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it, and "Reset progress" on the dive map erases your progress at any time.

Network

The app works fully offline and does not connect to any server.

Children

Shellwater is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the Shellwater project page.
